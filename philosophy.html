How can I help you?

“What else can I get for you?”

Jeannene grew up when a store greeted you at the door, walked you to the section, and took things to the fitting room. That was just how it was done. She teaches that same care, so a younger professional can use it on the account they already have.

A client who feels looked after stays. That is how the next job shows up, for the customer and for the person trying to earn the business.

“You take good care of me.”

That is what clients say when they stay. Not a program. Not a script. People stay because they feel looked after, and that is how sales grow.

She sees herself as the voice for the customer. The question she wants a younger person to learn is “How can I help you?” Said once, then done.
