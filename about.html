Meet Jeannene Meisman

Jeannene’s career is in fire protection, fire sprinkler and fire alarm, on the sales and relationship side of design, engineering, and consulting. People trust her because of the work, not a title. Clients stay because they tell her, “You take good care of me.” Last year she blew her sales goal away, by about double. Her boss told her she does “the hardest part”: generating the opportunities and the relationships.

Today she works at Summit Fire Consulting. She generates leads, builds opportunities, gathers the drawings and documents, and helps get proposals out the door.

Before that she was at Tyco, a larger company, where she had more budget and more room to move. She spent real money with the industry associations. The work was the same at the core: know the product, know the code, and take care of the person in front of you.

She has taught classes. She will stop a student who pulls the wrong information off the open internet. Use the NFPA source. Get it right. She knows the technical side well enough to teach it, and the sales side well enough to outperform.

She has been in AFSA, the American Fire Sprinkler Association, and NFSA, the National Fire Sprinkler Association, for a long time. Relationships with the executive directors. Negotiating memberships. National events and chapter events. Sponsoring. That is how you get in front of the people who make the decisions.

She is careful about who she introduces an important client to. Her name is what she is protecting. She will not spend it casually.

She also lived and worked in the Middle East for a period. Long days on the job site, then the U.S. calls, working across the time difference. You learn quickly who you are when the day does not end at five.

She grew up in California in the 1960s and early 1970s, in Los Angeles, San Francisco, and San Diego, because her father worked for NCR and the family moved when he was transferred. She lives in Florida now. This is home.
